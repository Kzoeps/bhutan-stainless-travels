# Pathfinder Local-First Copy Style Guide

## Voice definition

- Local: grounded in Bhutan places, people, and day-to-day realities.
- Practical: clear about what is included, what is flexible, and what happens next.
- Tailor-made: emphasizes custom planning and traveler preferences without sales hype.

## Required local experience references

- Include concrete, customizable examples such as Phobjikha homestays and Bumthang cooking lessons where relevant.
- Frame these as options that can be adjusted to fit timing, interests, and comfort.

## Allowed phrasing patterns

- "Tailor-made itineraries built around your pace and interests."
- "We shape each route with local guides and seasonal access in mind."
- "Use our suggested routes as a starting point, then adjust to fit your group."
- "Tell us your priorities and we will return a detailed, flexible plan."
- "Add experiences like a Phobjikha homestay or a Bumthang cooking lesson if they fit your trip."

## Banned phrasing patterns

- AI-sounding hype: "unforgettable", "once-in-a-lifetime", "world-class", "ultimate", "best ever".
- Unverifiable superlatives: "the most authentic", "the number one", "guaranteed".
- Fixed-price language: "flat rate", "fixed price", "all-inclusive price", "set package price".
- Em dash punctuation: do not use em dash characters in any copy.

## Explicit rules

- No fixed-price rule: never promise a fixed or universal price. Always position pricing as quote-based and tailored.
- No em dash rule: avoid em dash characters entirely. Use periods or commas instead.

## Before and after examples

Example 1

- Before: "Choose our ultimate Bhutan package for a once-in-a-lifetime journey."
- After: "Start with a suggested Bhutan route, then tailor the plan to your pace and interests."

Example 2

- Before: "All-inclusive price covers everything for the best Himalayan experience."
- After: "Share your priorities and we will send a quote that fits your dates, stays, and guide needs."

Example 3

- Before: "Our world-class team delivers the most authentic local stays."
- After: "Consider a Phobjikha homestay or a Bumthang cooking lesson, and we will fit the timing to your route."
